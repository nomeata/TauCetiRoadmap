# Roadmap: automorphisms of free groups and their linear images

**Summary.** This roadmap builds the basic theory of the automorphism group `Aut(F_n)` of a free
group of finite rank and of its natural representation on the abelianization. Its first headline
is Nielsen's theorem: `Aut(F_n)` is generated by the elementary Nielsen automorphisms, which
permute the basis, invert one basis element, or multiply one basis element by another. Its second
is the abelianization representation `B : Aut(F_n) → GL_n(ℤ)`, sending an automorphism to the
matrix of exponent sums of the images of the basis. `B` is surjective, its kernel `IA_n` contains
the inner automorphisms, and it descends to `Out(F_n) → GL_n(ℤ)`. Its third is a description of
the image under `B` of the stabilizer in `Aut(F_n)` of a finite-index subgroup `U`. Reduced mod 2,
the image fixes the vector recording which basis elements act by odd permutations on the cosets
of `U`. When `U` is the preimage of a sublattice `L ≤ ℤⁿ`, the image is exactly the stabilizer of
`L` in `GL_n(ℤ)`; index-two subgroups are an instance.

Two pieces of linear algebra over `ℤ` and over finite fields serve these results and are
developed for their own sake:
- Principal congruence subgroups `Γ_n(N)` of `GL_n(ℤ)`: the image of reduction mod `N` (all of
  `SL_n(ℤ/N)` from `SL_n(ℤ)`), explicit generators of `Γ_n(2)` by squares of elementary matrices
  and sign changes, the level of a congruence subgroup of `SL_n(ℤ)`, and stabilizers of
  sublattices of `ℤⁿ`.
- The stabilizer `S(v)` of a nonzero row vector `v` in `GL_n(K)` for a field `K`: its index
  `|K|ⁿ − 1` over a finite field, its maximality in `GL_n(𝔽₂)`, and explicit generators of it in
  `GL_n(𝔽₂)`.

Neighbours:
- **FreeGroupsAndLimitGroups** supplies the free-group background and owns primitive elements
  (a primitive element is the image of a basis element under some automorphism) and Stallings
  graphs. Nothing of its later layers is consumed.
- **InverseGaloisAndArithmeticFundamentalGroups** owns the action of `Aut(F_r)` on surjections
  `F_r ↠ G` (Nielsen equivalence and `T`-systems) and consumes the generation theorem.
- **KTheoryLowDegrees** and **AlgebraicKTheory** own the elementary subgroups `E_n(R)`, relative
  groups and the congruence subgroup property of `SL_n`. This roadmap consumes `E_n` and stays at
  the level of explicit congruence subgroups of `GL_n(ℤ)`.
- **NoncongruenceModularForms** and **ModularFormsPartII** own levels of finite-index subgroups of
  `SL₂(ℤ)` specifically; this roadmap's levels are for subgroups of `SL_n(ℤ)` for every `n` and
  are compared with theirs in a Check of §3.4.

How to read the build: Layer 1 is bases, automorphisms and Nielsen's generation theorem. Layer 2
is the abelianization representation `B`, its surjectivity and its kernel. Layer 3 is congruence
subgroups of `GL_n(ℤ)`. Layer 4 is vector stabilizers in `GL_n` over a field. Layer 5 combines
them: the action of `Aut(F_n)` on finite-index subgroups and the images of stabilizers under `B`.
Layers 3 and 4 do not depend on Layers 1 and 2.

## Conventions

- **The free group.** `ι` is a finite type with decidable equality, `F_ι` is Mathlib's
  `FreeGroup ι`, and `g_i = FreeGroup.of i` is its standard basis; the rank is
  `r = Fintype.card ι`. A general free group is a group `G` with `b : FreeGroupBasis ι G`; results
  for `G` are transported from `F_ι` along `b.repr : G ≃* FreeGroup ι` (Mathlib `MulAut.congr`),
  never proved twice.
- **Automorphisms** are `MulAut G`, with multiplication `(γ * δ) w = γ (δ w)`.
- **Action on subgroups.** `MulAut G` acts on `Subgroup G` by Mathlib's scoped
  `Subgroup.pointwiseMulAction`, `γ • U = U.map γ`. The stabilizer of `U` is
  `MulAction.stabilizer (MulAut G) U`, written `Stab(U)`.
- **Cosets.** Cosets are Mathlib's left cosets `G ⧸ U`, with `G` acting on the left:
  `π_U(w)(vU) = wvU`, which is `MulAction.toPermHom G (G ⧸ U)`.
- **Matrices.** `B(γ)_{ij}` is the exponent sum of `g_i` in `γ(g_j)`: the `j`-th column of `B(γ)`
  is the abelianization of `γ(g_j)`. With this convention `B` is a homomorphism, not an
  anti-homomorphism.
- **Elementary and sign matrices.** `X_{ij}(c) = 1 + c E_{ij}` for `i ≠ j` is Tau Ceti's
  `TauCeti.transvectionUnit`. `T_i` is the diagonal matrix with `-1` at `i` and `1` elsewhere.
- **Row vectors.** Row vectors act on the right: `v ↦ v ᵥ* M` (Mathlib `Matrix.vecMul`). For a
  row vector `v`, `S(v) = {M ∈ GL_n(K) | v ᵥ* M = v}`.
- **Congruence subgroups.** `Γ_ι(N)` is the kernel of reduction
  `Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup.map (Int.castRingHom (ZMod N)) : GL ι ℤ →* GL ι (ZMod N)`, for
  `N : ℕ`, and `Γ^SL_ι(N)` is its preimage under `Matrix.SpecialLinearGroup.toGL`. Level `0`
  gives `⊥` and level `1` gives `⊤`. A subgroup `H` of `GL_ι(ℤ)` or of `SL_ι(ℤ)` is a congruence
  subgroup when it contains `Γ_ι(N)`, respectively `Γ^SL_ι(N)`, for some `N ≥ 1`. Levels are
  defined for subgroups of `SL_ι(ℤ)` only: in `GL_ι(ℤ)`, `Γ_ι(3)` and `Γ_ι(4)` both lie in
  `SL_ι(ℤ)`, so the set of `N` with `Γ_ι(N) ≤ H` is not closed under `gcd`.
- **Mod-2 reduction of `B`.** `B̄(γ)` is the image of `B(γ)` in `GL_ι(ℤ/2)`.

## Layer 1: bases, automorphisms and Nielsen's theorem (S1)

### 1.1 Automorphisms and bases

For `b : FreeGroupBasis ι G` prove that `γ ↦ b.map γ` is a bijection from `MulAut G` onto the set
of bases indexed by `ι` (Mathlib `FreeGroupBasis.map`), and that for `G = F_ι` it sends `γ` to the
basis `i ↦ γ(g_i)`. Prove that an endomorphism `φ` of `F_ι`, `ι` finite, is an automorphism iff
the elements `φ(g_i)` generate `F_ι`: free groups of finite rank are Hopfian, by Tau Ceti's
`Group.ResiduallyFinite.injective_of_surjective` and `FreeGroup.instResiduallyFinite`. Name the
bijection `FreeGroupBasis.mulAutEquiv`. *Needs:* Mathlib `FreeGroupBasis`, `FreeGroup.lift`; Tau
Ceti `Group.ResiduallyFinite.injective_of_surjective`, `FreeGroup.instResiduallyFinite`.

**Checks.**

- For `ι = Fin 1`, `MulAut F_ι` has exactly two elements, the identity and `g ↦ g⁻¹`.
- The endomorphism of `F_{Fin 2}` with `g₀ ↦ g₀²`, `g₁ ↦ g₁` is injective but not an automorphism:
  its image misses `g₀`. Generation, not injectivity, is the criterion.

### 1.2 Elementary Nielsen automorphisms

Define, for `F_ι`:
- the permutation automorphisms `FreeGroup.freeGroupCongr σ` for `σ : Equiv.Perm ι` (Mathlib);
- the inversion `invAt i`, sending `g_i ↦ g_i⁻¹` and fixing the other `g_k`;
- the right multiplication `mulRight i j`, sending `g_i ↦ g_i g_j` for `i ≠ j` and fixing the
  other `g_k` (Tau Ceti `TauCeti.FreeGroup.nielsenMul` with exponent `1`);
- the left multiplication `mulLeft i j`, sending `g_i ↦ g_j g_i`.

Prove that each is an automorphism, compute their inverses and their values on every `g_k`, and
define `nielsenSubgroup ι` as the subgroup generated by the permutations, the inversions and the
right multiplications, and prove `mulLeft i j = τ * mulRight i j * τ` with `τ = invAt i * invAt j`,
so that the left multiplications lie in it. Tau Ceti's `TauCeti.FreeGroup.generatorInversion`,
which inverts every basis element, is the product of the `invAt i`. *Needs:* §1.1; Tau Ceti
`TauCeti.FreeGroup.nielsenMul`.

**Checks.**

- `mulRight i j` sends `g_i g_j⁻¹ ↦ g_i`.
- For `i ≠ j`, `(mulRight i j * mulRight j i) g_i = g_i g_j` while
  `(mulRight j i * mulRight i j) g_i = g_i g_j g_i`, so the two do not commute.
- `(invAt i * mulRight i j * invAt i) g_i = g_j⁻¹ g_i`: conjugating by `invAt i` alone does not
  give `mulLeft i j`.

### 1.3 Nielsen reduction of generating tuples

For a tuple `t : ι → F_ι`, an elementary Nielsen transformation replaces `t` by `t ∘ σ`, inverts
one entry, or replaces `t_i` by `t_i t_j` (`i ≠ j`). These correspond to composing the map
`FreeGroup.lift t` on the right with the automorphisms of §1.2. Define `IsNielsenReduced` by the conditions
N0–N2 of Lyndon–Schupp, for `u, v, w` ranging over entries and their inverses: N0, no entry is
trivial; N1, `|uv| ≥ |u|` and `|uv| ≥ |v|` whenever `uv ≠ 1`; N2, `|uvw| > |u| − |v| + |w|`
whenever `uv ≠ 1` and `vw ≠ 1`; lengths are the reduced lengths of Mathlib's `FreeGroup.toWord`.
Prove:
- every tuple is carried by finitely many elementary Nielsen transformations to a tuple whose
  nontrivial entries form a Nielsen-reduced tuple. The measure is the total length, with ties
  broken, when N2 fails without shortening, by Lyndon–Schupp's well-ordering on the left halves
  of the entries (lyndonSchupp77, Chapter I, Proposition 2.2);
- a Nielsen-reduced tuple has the length property: the reduced length of a product `u₁ ⋯ u_m` of
  entries and inverses with no adjacent `u_k u_{k+1} = 1` is at least `m`;
- consequently a Nielsen-reduced tuple of `|ι|` entries generating `F_ι` consists of the basis
  elements up to permutation and inversion.

(lyndonSchupp77, Chapter I, Propositions 2.2 and 2.5.) This `IsNielsenReduced` is the one
definition of Nielsen-reduced tuples; FreeGroupsAndLimitGroups §2.8, which produces
Nielsen-reduced bases from Stallings graphs, uses it if it needs the predicate. *Needs:* §1.2;
Mathlib `FreeGroup.toWord`, `FreeGroup.reduce`.

**Checks.**

- `(g₀ g₁, g₁)` in `F_{Fin 2}` is not Nielsen reduced (N1 fails for `u = g₀ g₁`, `v = g₁⁻¹`);
  three transformations (invert the second entry, multiply the first by it, invert it back) carry
  it to `(g₀, g₁)`.
- `(g₀², g₁)` is Nielsen reduced and generates a proper subgroup, so the last bullet needs the
  hypothesis that the tuple generates.

### 1.4 Nielsen's generation theorem

Prove `nielsenSubgroup ι = ⊤`: for finite `ι`, `MulAut F_ι` is generated by the elementary
Nielsen automorphisms. Transport it to any `G` with `b : FreeGroupBasis ι G`, the generators then
being defined on the basis `b`. *Proof sketch.* For `γ`, apply §1.3 to `t = (γ(g_i))_i`, which
generates `F_ι`; the transformations used compose `γ` with elementary automorphisms until the
tuple is a signed permutation of the basis, itself a product of permutations and inversions.
(nielsen24; lyndonSchupp77, Chapter I, Proposition 4.1.) *Needs:* §1.1, §1.3.

**Checks.**

- `invAt 0` alone is a Nielsen generator that is not a product of right multiplications and
  permutations: it reverses the orientation of the basis (see the determinant Check of §2.2).
- For `ι = Fin 1` the theorem says `MulAut ℤ` is generated by `invAt 0`.

### Examples

- `mulRight i j` and `invAt i` generate `MulAut F_{Fin 2}` together with the swap
  `freeGroupCongr (Equiv.swap 0 1)`.

### Dependencies

Mathlib `FreeGroup`, `FreeGroupBasis`, `FreeGroup.toWord`; Tau Ceti `TauCeti.FreeGroup.nielsenMul`,
`TauCeti.FreeGroup.generatorInversion`.

## Layer 2: the abelianization representation (S2)

### 2.1 Exponent sums

Define the exponent-sum homomorphism `expSum : F_ι →* Multiplicative (ι → ℤ)`, `g_i ↦ Pi.single i 1`,
and prove that it is surjective, that its kernel is the commutator subgroup, and that it induces
the identification of `Abelianization F_ι` with `ι → ℤ` compatible with Mathlib's
`FreeAbelianGroup ι` (`Abelianization (FreeGroup ι) ≃* Multiplicative (FreeAbelianGroup ι)` and
`FreeAbelianGroup.equivFinsupp`). Write `#_i w` for the `i`-th coordinate. *Needs:* Mathlib
`FreeGroup.lift`, `Abelianization`, `FreeAbelianGroup`.

**Checks.**

- `#_0 (g₀ g₁ g₀⁻¹) = 0` and `#_1 (g₀ g₁ g₀⁻¹) = 1`.
- `expSum [g₀, g₁] = 1`.

### 2.2 The homomorphism `B`

Define `autToGL : MulAut F_ι →* GL ι ℤ` with `(autToGL γ) i j = #_i (γ g_j)`, and prove the
transformation formula `expSum (γ w) = autToGL γ *ᵥ expSum w` (as vectors), from which
multiplicativity follows. Prove that `autToGL γ` is the matrix, in the basis of §2.1, of
`Abelianization.map γ`. For `b : FreeGroupBasis ι G` define
`FreeGroupBasis.autToGL b = autToGL ∘ MulAut.congr b.repr`, so that `(B_b γ) i j` is the exponent
sum of `b i` in `b.repr (γ (b j))`, and prove the change-of-basis formula
`B_{b.map δ} γ = B_b δ⁻¹ * B_b γ * B_b δ` (in the order fixed by the convention). *Needs:* §2.1,
§1.1; Mathlib `MulAut.congr`, `Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup`.

**Checks.**

- `autToGL (invAt i) = T_i`.
- `autToGL (mulRight i j) = X_{ji}(1)`: the `i`-th column becomes `e_i + e_j`, so the matrix is
  `1 + E_{ji}`. A definition putting images in rows would give `X_{ij}(1)` and an
  anti-homomorphism.
- `autToGL (freeGroupCongr σ)` has its `1` in position `(σ j, j)`: it is
  `Matrix.permMatrixHom ℤ σ`, not `σ.permMatrix ℤ`, which has its `1` in position `(i, σ i)`.
- For `ι = Fin 2` the automorphism `g₀ ↦ g₁`, `g₁ ↦ g₀ g₁` has matrix with columns `e₁` and
  `e₀ + e₁`, of determinant `-1`; right multiplications have determinant `1`, so every
  factorization of it into Nielsen generators (§1.4) uses an odd number of transpositions and
  inversions.

### 2.3 Surjectivity

Prove that `autToGL` is surjective. *Proof sketch.* By §2.2 its range contains every `X_{ij}(1)`,
hence every `X_{ij}(c)`, hence `SL_ι(ℤ)`: Tau Ceti's
`Matrix.SpecialLinearGroup.closure_range_toSpecialLinearGroup_eq_top` is stated for Mathlib's
`TransvectionStruct`, and `TauCeti.coe_transvectionUnit` identifies these with
`TauCeti.transvectionUnit`. It contains `T_i`, of determinant `-1`. This proof does not use §1.4.
*Needs:* §2.2; Tau Ceti `Matrix.SpecialLinearGroup.closure_range_toSpecialLinearGroup_eq_top`,
`TauCeti.coe_transvectionUnit`.

**Checks.**

- For `ι = Fin 1` the map is an isomorphism `MulAut ℤ ≃* GL (Fin 1) ℤ ≃* ℤˣ`.

### 2.4 The kernel `IA` and the outer quotient

Define `IA ι = (autToGL).ker`. Prove that `IA ι` contains every inner automorphism
(`MulAut.conj`), that `IA ι` is normal, and that `autToGL` factors through Tau Ceti's outer
automorphism group `OuterAut (FreeGroup ι)` of TeichmullerGeometry §4.2 (`MulAut G` modulo the
inner automorphisms) as a surjection `OuterAut (FreeGroup ι) →* GL ι ℤ`. Prove that the image of
`IA ι` in `OuterAut (FreeGroup ι)` is the kernel of this surjection. *Needs:* §2.3;
TeichmullerGeometry §4.2.

**Checks.**

- For `ι = Fin 3` the automorphism `g₀ ↦ g₀ [g₁, g₂]`, fixing `g₁` and `g₂`, lies in `IA` and is
  not inner: an inner automorphism fixing `g₁` and `g₂` is conjugation by an element of the
  centralizer of `⟨g₁, g₂⟩`, which is trivial.
- For `ι = Fin 1`, `IA = ⊥`.

### Examples

- The automorphism `g₀ ↦ g₁ g₀ g₁⁻¹`, `g₁ ↦ g₁` of `F_{Fin 2}` is inner, so it lies in `IA`.

### Dependencies

§1.1–§1.2; Mathlib `Abelianization`, `FreeAbelianGroup`, `MulAut.congr`; Tau Ceti
`TauCeti.transvectionUnit`, `TauCeti.coe_transvectionUnit`,
`Matrix.SpecialLinearGroup.closure_range_toSpecialLinearGroup_eq_top`; TeichmullerGeometry §4.2.

## Layer 3: congruence subgroups of `GL_n(ℤ)` (S3)

### 3.1 Principal congruence subgroups

Define `principalCongruenceGL ι N = Γ_ι(N)` as in the conventions. Prove that it is normal, that
`Γ_ι(0) = ⊥`, `Γ_ι(1) = ⊤`, that `Γ_ι(N) ≤ Γ_ι(M)` iff `M ∣ N` when `2 ≤ |ι|`, that
`Γ_ι(N) ⊓ Γ_ι(M) = Γ_ι(lcm N M)`, and that the preimage `Γ^SL_ι(N)` of `Γ_ι(N)` under
`Matrix.SpecialLinearGroup.toGL` is the kernel of `SL_ι(ℤ) → SL_ι(ℤ/N)`, which for `ι = Fin 2` is
Mathlib's `CongruenceSubgroup.Gamma N`. *Needs:* Mathlib `Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup.map`,
`CongruenceSubgroup.Gamma`.

**Checks.**

- `Γ_{Fin 1}(N)` is `⊥` for `N ≥ 3` and `⊤` for `N ∈ {1, 2}`: `GL₁(ℤ) = {±1}`. So
  `Γ_{Fin 1}(4) ≤ Γ_{Fin 1}(3)` although `3 ∤ 4`, which is why the divisibility criterion needs
  `2 ≤ |ι|`.
- `X_{01}(N) ∈ Γ_{Fin 2}(N)` and `X_{01}(1) ∉ Γ_{Fin 2}(N)` for `N ≥ 2`.

### 3.2 Images of reduction

Prove that `SL_ι(ℤ) → SL_ι(ℤ/N)` is surjective for every `N ≥ 1` and every finite `ι`, and that
the image of `GL_ι(ℤ) → GL_ι(ℤ/N)` is `{M | det M = ±1}`. In particular, for nonempty `ι`,
reduction mod `N` is surjective on `GL` iff every unit of `ℤ/N` is `±1`, that is
`N ∈ {1, 2, 3, 4, 6}`. *Proof sketch.* `ℤ/N` is finite, hence semilocal, hence of Bass stable
range one, so `SL_ι(ℤ/N) = E_ι(ℤ/N)` for `|ι| ≥ 2` (KTheoryLowDegrees §8.2); the elementary
subgroup of `ℤ` maps onto that of `ℤ/N` (AlgebraicKTheory A1 §1.4). For `|ι| ≤ 1` both groups are
trivial. Tau Ceti's `Matrix.SpecialLinearGroup.map_intCast_zmod_surjective` is the case
`ι = Fin 2`. *Needs:* §3.1; KTheoryLowDegrees §8.2; AlgebraicKTheory A1 §1.4.

**Checks.**

- For `N = 5` and `ι = Fin 1` the image of `GL₁(ℤ)` is `{±1}`, of index 2 in `(ℤ/5)ˣ`.
- For `N = 2` reduction `GL_ι(ℤ) → GL_ι(ℤ/2)` is surjective.

### 3.3 Generators of `Γ_n(2)`

Prove that `Γ_ι(2)` is generated by the squares `X_{ij}(2)`, `i ≠ j`, and the sign matrices `T_i`,
and that its intersection with `SL_ι(ℤ)` is generated by the `X_{ij}(2)` and the products
`T_i T_j`. *Proof sketch.* For `A ≡ 1 mod 2`, reduce each column with left multiplications by
`X_{pq}(±2)`: the diagonal entry is odd and the others are even, so it differs in absolute value
from every other entry; whenever the column has two nonzero entries it therefore has a nonzero
entry strictly smaller in absolute value than a largest one, and adding `±2` times the smaller
row to the larger strictly lowers the sum of absolute values of the column. A
column with a single nonzero entry has that entry `±1` by invertibility; fix the sign with `T_p`
and clear the row with right multiplications by `X_{pq}(2k)`. Each `T_i` normalizes the subgroup
generated by the `X_{ij}(2)`, which gives the `SL` form. (breitner11, Remark 6.) *Needs:* §3.1;
Tau Ceti `TauCeti.transvectionUnit`.

**Checks.**

- For `ι = Fin 2` the `SL` form says that `Γ(2) ⊆ SL₂(ℤ)` is generated by `X_{01}(2)`, `X_{10}(2)` and
  `-1 = T₀ T₁`, the classical description.
- `X_{01}(1) ∉ Γ_ι(2)`, so the squares cannot be replaced by the elementary matrices themselves.

### 3.4 Congruence subgroups and levels

For `H ≤ SL_ι(ℤ)` define `IsCongruenceSL H` (some `Γ^SL_ι(N) ≤ H` with `N ≥ 1`) and, for such `H`,
`levelSL H`, the least such `N`. Prove that `Γ^SL_ι(N) ≤ H` iff `H` is the preimage of its image in
`SL_ι(ℤ/N)`; that `Γ^SL_ι(M) ⊔ Γ^SL_ι(N) = Γ^SL_ι(gcd M N)`, hence `Γ^SL_ι(N) ≤ H` iff
`levelSL H ∣ N`; that conjugates have the same level; that the level of `H ⊓ K` divides
`lcm (levelSL H) (levelSL K)`; and that a supergroup of `H` has level dividing `levelSL H`. For
`H ≤ GL_ι(ℤ)` define `IsCongruenceGL H` by `Γ_ι(N) ≤ H` for some `N ≥ 1`, and prove it equivalent to
`IsCongruenceSL` of the preimage of `H` in `SL_ι(ℤ)`. *Proof sketch.* The `gcd` identity follows
from §3.2: for `A ∈ Γ^SL_ι(gcd M N)` choose, by the Chinese remainder theorem and surjectivity of
reduction mod `lcm M N`, a `B ∈ Γ^SL_ι(M)` with `B ≡ A` mod `N`; then `B⁻¹ A ∈ Γ^SL_ι(N)`. Tau
Ceti's `sup_ker_map_intCast_zmod_eq_top_of_coprime` is the coprime case. *Needs:* §3.1, §3.2.

**Checks.**

- `levelSL Γ^SL_ι(N) = N` for `N ≥ 1` and `2 ≤ |ι|`.
- For `ι = Fin 2`, `Γ^SL_ι(N)` is Mathlib's `CongruenceSubgroup.Gamma N`, and `levelSL` agrees with
  the level of NoncongruenceModularForms on the subgroups containing `-1`.
- In `GL_{Fin 2}(ℤ)`, the image of `SL₂(ℤ)` contains `Γ(3)` and `Γ(4)` but not `Γ(1)`: the `gcd`
  identity fails in `GL`, which is why levels are defined in `SL`.
- The level of the preimage in `SL_ι(ℤ)` of `S(v)` (§4.1) is `2` for `v ≠ 0` in `(ℤ/2)^ι` and
  `2 ≤ |ι|`.

### 3.5 Stabilizers of sublattices

For a subgroup `L ≤ ι → ℤ` of finite index, let `Stab(L) = {M | M *ᵥ L = L}` (column vectors).
Prove that `Γ_ι(e) ≤ Stab(L)` whenever `e • x ∈ L` for all `x`, for instance for `e` the exponent
of `(ι → ℤ) ⧸ L`, so `Stab(L)` is a congruence subgroup and the level of its preimage in
`SL_ι(ℤ)` divides `e`; and that for `L_N = {x | N ∣ x i₀}`, `N ≥ 1`, and `|ι| ≥ 2`, that level is
exactly `N`. *Needs:* §3.4.

**Checks.**

- `Stab(ι → ℤ) = ⊤`, level `1`.
- `L = 2 (ι → ℤ)` has `Stab(L) = ⊤`: the level can be smaller than the exponent `e = 2`.

### Examples

- `GL_{Fin 3}(ℤ) / Γ_{Fin 3}(2) ≃ GL₃(𝔽₂)`, of order `168`.

### Dependencies

Mathlib `Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup.map`, `ZMod`, `CongruenceSubgroup.Gamma`; Tau Ceti
`TauCeti.transvectionUnit`, `Matrix.SpecialLinearGroup.map_intCast_zmod_surjective`,
`sup_ker_map_intCast_zmod_eq_top_of_coprime`; AlgebraicKTheory A1.

## Layer 4: vector stabilizers in `GL_n` over a field (S4)

### 4.1 The stabilizer of a row vector

For a field `K` and `v : ι → K`, define `vecStabilizer v = S(v)` as in the conventions, and prove
that it is a subgroup, that `S(0) = ⊤`, that `S(v)` is the stabilizer of `v` for the right action
of `GL_ι(K)` on row vectors, and that `M ↦ (Mᵀ)⁻¹` carries it onto the stabilizer of the column vector `v` for the action
`x ↦ M *ᵥ x`. *Needs:* Mathlib
`Matrix.vecMul`, `Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup`.

**Checks.**

- `X_{ij}(c) ∈ S(v)` iff `c v_i = 0`.
- `X_{ik}(1) X_{jk}(1) ∈ S(v)` over `𝔽₂` iff `v_i = v_j` (for `i, j, k` distinct).

### 4.2 Transitivity, conjugacy and index

Prove that `GL_ι(K)` acts transitively on nonzero row vectors, so that the `S(v)`, `v ≠ 0`, are
conjugate, `S(v M) = M⁻¹ S(v) M`; and that for finite `K` with `q` elements and `v ≠ 0`,
`[GL_ι(K) : S(v)] = qⁿ − 1`, `n = |ι|`. Tau Ceti's nonzero-vector results are for the column
action `x ↦ M *ᵥ x`; transfer them along the transpose-inverse of §4.1. *Needs:* §4.1; Tau Ceti
`TauCeti.nonzeroVectors`,
`TauCeti.isPretransitive_nonzeroVectors`, `TauCeti.natCard_nonzeroVectors`.

**Checks.**

- Over `𝔽₂` with `n = 3`, `[GL₃(𝔽₂) : S(v)] = 7`, so `|S(v)| = 24`.

### 4.3 Generators over `𝔽₂`

Prove that for `|ι| ≥ 3` and `v : ι → ℤ/2`, `S(v) ≤ GL_ι(ℤ/2)` is generated by the `X_{ij}(1)` with
`v_i = 0` and the products `X_{ik}(1) X_{jk}(1)` with `i, j, k` distinct and `v_i = v_j = 1`.
*Proof sketch.* Reduce `M ∈ S(v)` to the identity column by column with left multiplications.
Rows outside the columns already reduced may be added to any row. To make the diagonal entry
`1`, add a suitable row, together with a second row of `v`-weight one when the target row has
`v`-weight one; when no such second row exists, `v` has exactly two nonzero entries `v_i = v_j = 1`
and the swap of rows `i` and `j` is `(X_{ik}(1) X_{jk}(1) X_{ki}(1) X_{kj}(1))²` for any third `k`.
The remaining entries of the column to be cleared that lie in rows of `v`-weight one are even in
number because `v ᵥ* M = v`, so they are cleared in pairs. (breitner11, Lemma 8.) *Needs:* §4.1.

**Checks.**

- For `|ι| = 2` and `v = (1, 1)` over `𝔽₂` the products of the second kind do not exist and the
  first kind is empty, while `S(v)` contains the swap matrix: the hypothesis `|ι| ≥ 3` is
  needed for the explicit generating set.

### 4.4 Maximality

Prove that for `v ≠ 0` and `|ι| ≥ 2`, `S(v)` is a maximal subgroup of `GL_ι(𝔽₂)`; more generally,
over any field and for `|ι| ≥ 2` the stabilizer of a point of the projective space `ℙ(K^ι)` is
maximal in `GL_ι(K)`; the row-vector form follows by the transpose-inverse automorphism. For `|K| > 2` the vector stabilizer `S(v)` is strictly contained in the stabilizer of
the line `Kv`, hence not maximal. *Proof sketch.* `GL_ι(K)` acts 2-transitively on `ℙ(K^ι)`
(Mathlib `Projectivization.generalLinearGroup_is_two_pretransitive`), so the action is
preprimitive and point stabilizers are coatoms
(`MulAction.isCoatom_stabilizer_iff_preprimitive`); over `𝔽₂` vectors and points coincide.
*Needs:* §4.1; Mathlib `Projectivization`, `MulAction.IsPreprimitive`.

**Checks.**

- `GL₂(𝔽₂) ≅ S₃` and `S(v)` has order `2`, a maximal subgroup of `S₃`.
- For `K = 𝔽₃`, `S(e₀) < stabilizer of the line 𝔽₃ e₀ < GL₂(𝔽₃)`.

### Dependencies

Mathlib `Matrix.vecMul`, `Projectivization`, `MulAction.IsPreprimitive`; Tau Ceti

## Layer 5: `Aut(F_n)` and finite-index subgroups (S5)

### 5.1 Stabilizers of subgroups

For any group `G` and `γ ∈ MulAut G`, prove `γ ∈ Stab(U) ↔ ∀ g, γ g ∈ U ↔ g ∈ U`, and
`(γ • U).index = U.index`, `(γ • U).Normal ↔ U.Normal` and `(γ • U).normalCore = γ • U.normalCore`.
Prove that for finitely generated `G` and `n ≠ 0` the set of subgroups of index `n` is finite
(M. Hall's finiteness), so that for `U` of finite index `Stab(U)` has finite index in `MulAut G`.
*Proof sketch for finiteness.* A subgroup of index `n` is the stabilizer of a point for an action
of `G` on `Fin n`, given by a homomorphism `G →* Equiv.Perm (Fin n)`, and there are finitely many
of these (Tau Ceti `Monoid.FG.finite_monoidHom`). *Needs:* Mathlib `Subgroup.pointwiseMulAction`,
`MulAction.stabilizer`, `Subgroup.index`; Tau Ceti `Monoid.FG.finite_monoidHom`.

**Checks.**

- `F_{Fin 2}` has exactly three subgroups of index 2.
- A characteristic subgroup has `Stab(U) = ⊤`; the commutator subgroup of `F_ι` is an example.

### 5.2 Stabilizing automorphisms permute cosets

For `γ ∈ Stab(U)` define the permutation `cosetPerm γ` of `G ⧸ U`, `vU ↦ γ(v)U`, and prove
`π_U(γ w) = cosetPerm γ * π_U(w) * (cosetPerm γ)⁻¹`, that `cosetPerm` is a homomorphism
`Stab(U) →* Perm (G ⧸ U)`, and that `cosetPerm γ` fixes the coset `U`. Prove the converse: if some
permutation fixing `U` conjugates `π_U ∘ γ` to `π_U`, then `γ ∈ Stab(U)`. (breitner11, Remark 3.)
*Needs:* §5.1; Mathlib `MulAction.toPermHom`.

**Checks.**

- For `U` normal, `cosetPerm γ` is the automorphism of `G ⧸ U` induced by `γ` (Mathlib
  `QuotientGroup.congr`).

### 5.3 The parity bound

For `U ≤ F_ι` of finite index define `signVec U : ι → ℤ/2`, `1` where `π_U(g_i)` is an odd
permutation. Prove that `B̄(γ) ∈ S(signVec U)` for every `γ ∈ Stab(U)`. Equivalently,
`signVec U ᵥ* B̄(γ) = signVec U`. *Proof sketch.* `Equiv.Perm.sign ∘ π_U` is a homomorphism to
`ℤˣ`, determined by `signVec U` through exponent sums; by §5.2 it is invariant under `γ`.
(breitner11, Proposition 16.) *Needs:* §2.2, §4.1, §5.2.

**Checks.**

- For `U = ⊤`, `signVec U = 0` and the bound is empty.
- For the kernel `U` of `F_{Fin 2} → ℤ/3`, `g₀ ↦ 1`, `g₁ ↦ 0`, `signVec U = 0` (a 3-cycle is
  even), while `B(Stab U)` is the stabilizer of the lattice `{x | 3 ∣ x₀}` (§5.4): the parity bound
  can be far from sharp.

### 5.4 Preimages of sublattices

For a subgroup `L ≤ ι → ℤ` and `U = expSum⁻¹(L)`, prove
`autToGL '' Stab(U) = Stab(L)` (§3.5). *Proof sketch.* `ker expSum` is characteristic, so `γ`
induces `autToGL γ` on `ι → ℤ` (§2.2) and `γ ∈ Stab(U) ↔ autToGL γ ∈ Stab(L)`; surjectivity
(§2.3) gives every element of `Stab(L)`. (breitner11, Remark 17.) *Needs:* §2.3, §3.5, §5.1.

**Checks.**

- **Index-two subgroups.** A subgroup `U ≤ F_ι` of index 2 is `expSum⁻¹(L)` for the lattice
  `L = {x | v ⬝ᵥ x ≡ 0 mod 2}`, `v_i = 1` iff `g_i ∉ U`. Hence `B(Stab U)` is the preimage of
  `S(v)` in `GL_ι(ℤ)`, and `B̄(Stab U) = S(v)` (§3.2 with `N = 2`), matching §5.3 with equality.
- `L = {x | N ∣ x i₀}` with `2 ≤ |ι|`: `B(Stab U)` is a congruence subgroup whose preimage in
  `SL_ι(ℤ)` has level exactly `N` (§3.5).

### Examples

- The loop subgroups of breitner11 are in general not preimages of sublattices. For a loop
  subgroup with `r ≥ 3` and at most `r − 2` loops of length one, `B(Stab U)` is the full preimage of
  `S(signVec U)`, so the bound of §5.3 is attained; for the `3/3/1` loop subgroup of `F₃`
  (index 5) it is all of `GL₃(ℤ)`. This is a downstream application, not a target of this
  roadmap.

`Equiv.Perm.sign`.
